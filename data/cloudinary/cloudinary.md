## Overview

Cloudinary provides image upload, manipulation, storage, and delivery for websites and applications with libraries for Ruby, Python, Java, PHP, Objective-C, and more.

## Features

- Image upload and storage
- Powerful image manipulations and transformations
- CDN delivery
- SDKs for multiple programming languages

## Pricing

Freemium model. The free tier includes 25 monthly credits. One credit equals 1,000 image transformations, 1 GB of storage, or 1 GB of CDN usage.
