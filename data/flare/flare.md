## Overview

Flare is a nonbloated, modern, and highly configurable file/screenshot vault server. It supports ShareX, Flameshot, and Spectacle clients, and offers OCR search functionality.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via Docker. Built with Node.js.

- OCR search
