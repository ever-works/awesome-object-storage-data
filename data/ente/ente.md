## Overview

Ente is an end-to-end encrypted cloud service for photos, videos, and 2FA secrets. It can also be self-hosted.

## Features

- End-to-end encryption
- Photo, video, and 2FA secret storage
- Self-hostable
- Forever free tier of 10 GB
- Single replica of data for free tier users

## Pricing

Free tier includes 10 GB of storage forever.
