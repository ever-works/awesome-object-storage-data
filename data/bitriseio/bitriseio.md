## Overview

bitrise.io is a CI/CD platform for mobile apps, supporting native or hybrid applications.

## Features

- CI/CD for mobile apps (native or hybrid)
- 200 free builds/month with 10 min build time
- Two team members on free plan
- OSS projects: 45 min build time, +1 concurrency, unlimited team size

## Pricing

Freemium: 200 free builds/month. OSS projects get additional benefits.
