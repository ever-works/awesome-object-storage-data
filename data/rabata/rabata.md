## Overview

Rabata is an S3-compatible object storage service providing full S3 API compatibility. It offers two storage tiers with a max object size of 5 TB. Backup plans are billed in 10 TB increments. No free egress allowance.

## Pricing

Paid. Storage at $0.010/GB/mo. Egress at $0.01/GB with no free egress.

## Features

- Full S3 compatibility
- Two storage tiers
- Max object size: 5 TB
- Backup plans billed in 10 TB increments
- No free egress
