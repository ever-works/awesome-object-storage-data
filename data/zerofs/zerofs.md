## Overview

ZeroFS is a log-structured filesystem for S3. It serves S3-compatible buckets as POSIX filesystems over NFS and 9P, or as raw block devices over NBD.

## Key Features

- Log-structured filesystem for S3
- Exposes S3 buckets as POSIX filesystems over NFS and 9P
- Exposes S3 buckets as raw block devices over NBD
- S3-compatible backend
